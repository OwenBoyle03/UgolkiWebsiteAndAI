How to play:

Pieces can move in any of the four cardinal directions: up, down, left or right as long as there is an open space.

If there is a piece in front of the piece you wish to move then you can jump over it if there is an empty space after it.

To win the game you must move your pieces to the opponents corner in the same formation they started in.
